# 20 Hours to Mastery

A hi-fi practice timer. Pick a mission, press **Let's go**, and bank your minutes until you reach 20 hours: a star at 5, 10 and 15 hours, and a trophy at 20.

- Installs to your phone's home screen and works offline.
- Your progress is saved on the device you use. Use **Settings → Save a backup** now and then, and **Restore** to move to a new phone.
- No accounts, no tracking, no network calls.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
